# Privacy policy for txt

Last updated: June 24, 2026.

txt is an offline messaging app for Android.
It sends pictures, QR codes, live location and payment requests over plain SMS.
This policy describes every kind of data the app handles.

## What txt does not do

- txt has no servers.
- txt has no accounts.
- txt has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- txt does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

## What stays on the device

Transfer records, favorite contacts and app settings are stored in a local database on your phone.
Deleting the app deletes this data.

## What leaves the device

Only the SMS you choose to send.
They travel through your mobile carrier to the recipient you picked.
SMS is not encrypted, so write accordingly.

## Permissions

- SMS: txt sends your messages and reads the inbox so multipart incoming items can be reassembled.
- Notifications: used to alert you about new incoming items.
- Camera: reads QR codes you point it at.
- Location: read only while a location share is running.
- Contacts: read only when you pick a recipient or a favorite.
