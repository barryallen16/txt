### What's New in v1.0.1

* **Pay to Mobile Number & UPI ID:** Enter any 10-digit mobile number or VPA to generate an SMS payment request without scanning a physical QR code.
* **Preserve Merchant QR Signatures:** Signed merchant QR codes now preserve their exact parameter ordering and base64 signatures byte-for-byte, preventing banking verification failures.
* **Sent Items & Transfer History:** View received and sent transfers separately with live part counters and status indicators.
* **Direct UPI Intent Queries:** Registered Android package query intents to allow direct launching of installed UPI applications.
* **Location Sharing Intervals:** Added selectable intervals (30s, 1m, 5m) for real-time offline location SMS broadcasts.

### Installation

1. Download **`txt-1.0.1.apk`** (or **`txt.apk`**).
2. Open the APK on your Android phone (Android 8.0+).
3. If prompted by Play Protect or Restricted Settings, refer to the [Installation Guide](https://txt.runs-on.dev/#guide).
